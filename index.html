Reporting an Issue

To help us investigate, please include:

  • A description of the issue and the steps to reproduce it
  • The app name and version
  • Your device model and operating system version
  • Screenshots or a screen recording, if available

Please do not include passwords, verification codes, or other sensitive information.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do if the app isn’t working properly?

Try closing and reopening the app, then check the App Store for updates. If the issue continues, please contact us using the support email above.

How can I suggest a feature?

Send us an email describing what you’d like to do and how the feature would help. Your feedback helps us improve the app.

Should I uninstall the app if I encounter an issue?

Please contact us before uninstalling the app. Uninstalling may delete data stored only on your device. Keeping the app installed can help with troubleshooting.
